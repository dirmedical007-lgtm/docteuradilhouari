Sports medicine in Bouskoura

Dr Adil Houari, a sports medicine specialist recognised by the National Council of the Order of Physicians (CNOM) in December 2025, sees recreational and competitive athletes at his practice in Bouskoura.

Book an appointment
01

When should you consult?

For a medical assessment before starting sport, exercise-related pain, an injury or returning to sport after a break. The consultation may also cover training, recovery and nutrition.

02

What happens during the consultation?

The doctor reviews your medical history, symptoms and sporting goals, then performs an appropriate clinical examination. Musculoskeletal ultrasound or an ECG may be considered when medically indicated. A fitness certificate depends on the results of the assessment.

03

Preparing for your appointment

Bring available test results and medical reports, your usual treatments and, where applicable, the form required by your club or federation.

04

Qualifications

University diploma in sports medicine (University of Bordeaux), FIFA Diploma in Football Medicine (2023), recognition as a sports medicine specialist by the CNOM (2025).
