Consultations, tests and procedures

General Medicine

Complete medical care for the whole family, from infants to the elderly. Consultations for all your general health needs.

Diabetes and prediabetes follow-up

Prediabetes screening and diabetes follow-up: blood glucose and HbA1c monitoring, treatment support, nutrition and lifestyle advice, and complication prevention tailored to each patient.

Continuing education: American Diabetes Association (ADA), “Diabetes is Primary CE Certificate Program 2025”, certificate of completion dated November 23, 2025 (11 COP credits).
