Frequently asked questions

Where can I get an allergy test in Bouskoura?
Dr Houari Adil performs prick tests directly at his practice in Bouskoura, with no need to travel to Casablanca. Book an appointment on +212 7 17 17 30 88.
Which doctor should I see for an allergy in Bouskoura?
Dr Houari Adil, a general practitioner in Bouskoura with expertise in allergology, provides allergy assessment and monitoring for respiratory allergies. He performs prick tests at the practice and refers to a specialist when necessary.
What is a prick test?
A prick test is a quick skin test: small drops of allergen extracts (pollens, dust mites, cat dander…) are placed on the forearm and the skin is lightly pricked. A local reaction indicates sensitisation to the allergen. Results in 15 to 20 minutes.
Are prick tests painful?
No. The procedure is quick and minimally invasive, well tolerated by both adults and children.
