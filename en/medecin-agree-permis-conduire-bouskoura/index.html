The Driver's Licence Medical Examination

The medical examination is a mandatory step when obtaining or renewing a driver's licence. It ensures that the candidate meets the physical and sensory requirements needed to drive safely.

At Dr Houari Adil's practice, the examination includes:

👁️

Visual acuity

Assessment of distance vision, visual field and colour discrimination (colour blindness).

👂

Hearing

Verification of hearing to ensure correct perception of auditory signals.

🫀

Cardiovascular assessment

Auscultation and blood pressure measurement to detect any contraindications.

🧠

Neurological and motor assessment

Evaluation of reflexes, coordination and limb mobility.

The doctor may request further tests if the clinical situation warrants it. The certificate is issued immediately if fitness to drive is confirmed.

When should you book a medical?

🆕

First-time licence

For candidates sitting their driving test for the first time, the medical examination is required before registering at a driving school or during the administrative process.

🔄

Renewal

A driver's licence must be renewed periodically. A new medical examination is required to confirm the driver's continued fitness to drive.

🚛

Professional licence

Professional drivers (passenger transport, heavy goods vehicles) may be subject to specific medical checks depending on their activity.

If you are unsure which category applies to you, feel free to call the practice before your appointment.

Documents to bring

  • Valid national ID card (CIN) + photocopy
  • Current driver's licence + photocopy (for renewal only)
  • 3 recent identity photos, colour, full face, 35 × 45 mm, blue background

The doctor will inform you at the time of booking if any additional documents are required for your particular situation.

Frequently asked questions

Who can carry out the medical examination for a driver's licence in Morocco?
The medical examination must be carried out by a doctor approved by the competent authorities. Dr Houari Adil is a general practitioner who performs driver's licence medical examinations at his practice in Bouskoura.
What documents should I bring for the driver's licence medical?
Bring your valid national ID card (CIN) and a photocopy, 3 recent identity photos (colour, full face, 35 × 45 mm, blue background), and if renewing, your current driver's licence and a photocopy.
How quickly is the medical certificate issued?
The medical certificate is issued on the same day, immediately after the clinical examination, if the doctor confirms medical fitness to drive.
Is the practice accessible from Ville Verte and other communes in Nouaceur Prefecture?
Yes. Bouskoura and Ville Verte are both part of Nouaceur Prefecture. The practice, located in Bouskoura, is easily accessible from Ville Verte and the other communes of the prefecture.
Do I need to fast before the medical examination?
No, there are no dietary restrictions for this examination. Simply bring your documents and arrive on time for your appointment.
