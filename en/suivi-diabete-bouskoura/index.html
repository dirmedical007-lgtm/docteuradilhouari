Diabetes follow-up in Bouskoura

Dr Adil Houari provides prediabetes screening and diabetes follow-up as part of general medicine consultations at his practice in Bouskoura.

Book an appointment
01

When should you book an appointment?

To discuss an abnormal blood glucose result, review previously diagnosed diabetes or organise follow-up of your treatment and tests. The doctor may refer you to a specialist if necessary.

02

What does follow-up include?

Depending on your situation, the consultation may include reviewing blood glucose and HbA1c results, measuring blood pressure, reviewing treatment, nutrition advice and prevention of complications. The frequency of tests is decided with your doctor.

03

What should you bring?

Recent laboratory results, prescriptions, available medical reports and any blood glucose records.

04
